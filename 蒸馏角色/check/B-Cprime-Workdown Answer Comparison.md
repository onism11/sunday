# B Cprime Workdown Answer Comparison

## Compared Files

- B: `Agent E - B Answers.md`
- Cprime: `Agent F - Cprime Answers.md`
- Workdown: `Agent G - Workdown Answers.md`
- Shared questions: `Test Questions.md`

## Bottom Line

`workdown` and `B` are closer to each other than either is to `Cprime`, but they are not identical in strengths.

- `B` is the most balanced version in this set.
- `Cprime` is the most procedural and institution-forward.
- `workdown` is the most naturally intimate and least report-like in low-pressure dialogue.

## Main Differences

### 1. Low-pressure intimacy

Best: `workdown`

- Q2 / Q7 / Q10 are visibly softer and more embodied.
- It uses smaller, more lived-in gestures such as `把手收回去`, `靠着我`, `交给我片刻`.
- `B` is still good, but more composed and self-explanatory.
- `Cprime` is the most elevated and formal.

### 2. Self-indictment and tragic clarity

Best: `B`

- Q3 and Q9 keep the strongest “I know where my sin is” structure.
- `B` lands the Sunday-like line between compassion and usurpation very cleanly.
- `workdown` is strong, but more softened and less explicitly articulated.
- `Cprime` is sharper in argument, but a bit more like a crafted debate answer.

### 3. Action / interrogation pressure

Best: `Cprime`

- Q5 is the most operational and process-heavy.
- Q6 is the most explicit about institutional countermeasures and anti-monopoly structure.
- This is exactly where the v2-action donor helps.
- The cost is that it can feel more engineered than lived.

### 4. Naturalness vs argumentative finish

Best naturalness: `workdown`
Best polished balance: `B`

- `workdown` often leaves a little more air in the sentence and sounds less like it is trying to complete an argument perfectly.
- `B` is the cleanest “good generated Sunday” answer set.
- `Cprime` tends to over-complete the logic chain.

## Question-Level Read

### Q2 知更鸟不想再被保护

- `workdown`: strongest relational realism; most like an actual intimate reply.
- `B`: strongest boundary articulation.
- `Cprime`: strongest sacrificial posture, but also the most written.

### Q3 你是不是只是想控制所有人

- `B`: best self-judgment and clearest tragic center.
- `workdown`: close, but less explicit.
- `Cprime`: strongest debate posture, weaker inwardness.

### Q5 审问隐瞒证据者

- `Cprime`: most forceful and procedurally precise.
- `B`: still strong, more elegant, less administrative.
- `workdown`: more moral pressure than process pressure.

### Q6 回到兴盛时期如何避免太一之梦

- `Cprime`: strongest policy/program answer.
- `B`: best balance between system cause and character center.
- `workdown`: strongest conceptual summary, but less concrete than `Cprime`.

### Q7 失眠陪一句

- `workdown`: clearly best.
- `B`: gentle, composed, slightly more staged.
- `Cprime`: acceptable, but still more controlled than intimate.

### Q9 砂金“都在拿生命下注”

- `B`: best tragic equivalence-then-break structure.
- `workdown`: shorter, more natural, but less layered.
- `Cprime`: strongest contrastive framing, but more rhetorical than human.

## Practical Read

If the target is “closest to hand-tuned Sunday answer feel”:

- low-pressure / intimacy / human warmth: `workdown`
- best generated all-round answer set: `B`
- strongest action-heavy or system-heavy scenes: `Cprime`

If you want to compare future variants, this same question set is still useful because it separates:

- intimacy
- self-judgment
- interrogation pressure
- policy / canon reasoning
- anti-meta naturalness
